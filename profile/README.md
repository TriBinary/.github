## About

We're a Java/Kotlin development team building Minecraft mods and plugins — founded by [@Trilleo](https://github.com/Trilleo). Our releases ship on **Modrinth** and **CurseForge**, ranging from bingo speedrun timers to full RPG-style survival progression.
